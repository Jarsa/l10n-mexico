This module reads the CFDI payment complements (``pago20:Pagos``)
downloaded from the SAT by ``l10n_mx_sat`` and applies them to the
invoices they pay: the payment already registered in Odoo is linked to
the complement, or a new payment is created and reconciled with the
invoices when none exists.

The bank journal of a new payment is found through the bank account of
the company written in the complement (``CtaBeneficiario`` for issued
complements, ``CtaOrdenante`` for received ones), with an optional
fallback journal.

Configuration
=============

In the **SAT** tab of the company (Settings > Companies):

- **Payment complements**: *Do nothing*, *Link existing payments* or
  *Link existing payments or create them*.
- **Fallback bank journal**: journal of the payments whose bank account
  in the complement does not match a bank account of the company.
- **Apply payment complements on download**: apply each complement as
  soon as it is downloaded; the ones that cannot be applied stay
  pending.

The bank accounts of the company (Contacts > company > bank accounts)
and the bank journals linked to them are used to match the account of
the complement.

Usage
=====

Open **SAT > Documents** and filter *Payment complements to apply*. Each
complement shows its payments (``Pago`` nodes) and the documents they
pay, with the invoice found for each UUID. **Apply Payment** links or
creates the payment; the same action is available for several documents
from the list.

A complement is applied only when all its related invoices are
registered (for example with ``l10n_mx_sat_invoice``) and posted. The
payment is linked to the SAT document and shows it in its form.
