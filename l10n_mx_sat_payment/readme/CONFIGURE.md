In the **SAT** tab of the company (Settings > Companies):

- **Payment complements**: *Do nothing*, *Link existing payments* or *Link
  existing payments or create them*.
- **Fallback bank journal**: journal of the payments whose bank account in the
  complement does not match a bank account of the company.
- **Apply payment complements on download**: apply each complement as soon as
  it is downloaded; the ones that cannot be applied stay pending.

The bank accounts of the company (Contacts > company > bank accounts) and the
bank journals linked to them are used to match the account of the complement.
