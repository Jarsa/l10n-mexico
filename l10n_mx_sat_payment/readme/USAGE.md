Open **SAT > Documents** and filter *Payment complements to apply*. Each
complement shows its payments (`Pago` nodes) and the documents they pay, with
the invoice found for each UUID. **Apply Payment** links or creates the
payment; the same action is available for several documents from the list.

A complement is applied only when all its related invoices are registered
(for example with `l10n_mx_sat_invoice`) and posted. The payment is linked
to the SAT document and shows it in its form.
