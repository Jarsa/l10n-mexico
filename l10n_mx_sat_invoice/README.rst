This module creates customer invoices and vendor bills from the CFDIs
downloaded from the SAT by ``l10n_mx_sat``, without the Enterprise
Mexican localization.

From the list or the form of **SAT > Documents**, **Create Invoice**
opens a wizard that asks for the journal and the account and creates one
draft invoice per CFDI:

- Issued CFDIs become customer invoices or credit notes, received CFDIs
  become vendor bills or refunds, according to the voucher type (I or
  E).
- The partner is found by RFC (or created), the currency, date, series
  and folio are taken from the CFDI and the XML is attached to the
  invoice.
- Each concept becomes a line with its product (matched by code or
  name), its income/expense account or the account of the wizard, its
  discount and the Odoo taxes matching the CFDI taxes (SAT tax type,
  factor type and rate), withholdings included.
- Fuel concepts, whose IEPS is not broken down, are split in a line with
  the VAT base and an untaxed line with the IEPS; the same applies to
  the operations of a fuel account statement complement (ECC 1.2).
- Concepts where the IEPS applies to part of the base (e.g.
  telecommunications) are split in a line with IEPS and VAT and a line
  with VAT only.
- Local taxes (ImpuestosLocales complement) become untaxed lines.
- The chatter warns when the total of the invoice differs from the CFDI
  and lists the related CFDIs of credit notes.

The SAT document keeps a link to the invoice so a CFDI cannot be
imported twice. The Enterprise localization (``l10n_mx_edi``) is
supported by the bridge module ``l10n_mx_sat_invoice_enterprise``.

Configuration
=============

Configure the FIEL credentials and the download of issued and received
CFDIs in ``l10n_mx_sat`` (**Settings > Companies > SAT** tab).

The taxes of the company must have the **SAT Tax Type** (IVA, ISR,
IEPS), the **Factor Type** (Tasa, Cuota, Exento) and the rate of the
CFDI taxes; the Mexican chart of accounts provides them. The default
sale and purchase taxes of the company are preferred when several taxes
match.

Products are matched by internal reference (``NoIdentificacion``) or
name; their income/expense account is used when set, otherwise the
account chosen in the wizard.

Usage
=====

1. Go to **SAT > Documents** and filter the CFDIs **Without invoice**.
2. Select the CFDIs of one direction (issued or received) and choose
   **Actions > Create Invoices**, or open a CFDI and click **Create
   Invoice**.
3. Check the journal and the account proposed by the wizard and click
   **Create Invoices**. The account is used by the lines whose product
   has no income/expense account and by the local taxes.
4. Review the draft invoices: the chatter tells when the total differs
   from the CFDI, and the **SAT CFDI** field of the invoice links back
   to the document.

Only valid CFDIs of type I (invoice) or E (credit note) with XML and
without invoice can be imported. A missing tax (for instance a
withholding not configured) stops the import with a message telling
which tax to configure.
