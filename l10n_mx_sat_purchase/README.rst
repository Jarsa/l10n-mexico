This module links the CFDIs downloaded from the SAT by ``l10n_mx_sat``
with purchase orders.

A new **Bill from SAT** button on confirmed purchase orders opens a
wizard listing the received CFDIs of the vendor that are not yet linked
to a vendor bill. Once a CFDI is selected, its concepts are shown and
mapped to the lines of the purchase orders (several orders of the same
vendor can be billed from one CFDI), so the vendor bill is created with
the quantities, prices and discounts of the CFDI on the lines of the
orders. The XML is attached to the bill, so the standard CFDI import of
the accounting app can process it, and the SAT document keeps a
reference to the bill so it cannot be used twice.

The module also provides the abstract model
``l10n_mx_sat.document.selector.mixin`` so other modules can offer the
same CFDI selection on their own records.

Configuration
=============

Configure the FIEL credentials and the download of received CFDIs in
``l10n_mx_sat`` (**Settings > Companies > SAT** tab).

The vendor must have the RFC of the CFDI issuer in its **Tax ID** field,
and the company RFC must match the receiver of the CFDI.

Usage
=====

1. Open a confirmed purchase order and click **Bill from SAT**.
2. The **SAT CFDIs** tab lists the received CFDIs of the vendor that are
   pending to bill: folio, date, issuer, total, currency and SAT status.
   Only CFDIs in the currency of the order, not cancelled and not linked
   to another bill are proposed. When exactly one CFDI matches the
   orders total it comes pre-selected.
3. Tick the CFDI that corresponds to the order. The **Concepts** tab
   shows one line per concept of the CFDI (vendor code, description, SAT
   product code, quantity, SAT unit, unit price, amount, discount and
   taxes) and proposes the purchase order line it corresponds to,
   looking for, in this order: the ``NoIdentificacion`` of the concept
   equal to the internal reference of the product or to its vendor code,
   the description equal to the one of the line or of the product
   (ignoring case and accents), and a single line with the same unit
   price. When several lines match, the one of the oldest order with a
   pending quantity wins.
4. When the CFDI covers several purchase orders, add them in **Purchase
   Orders** (only confirmed orders of the same vendor and currency with
   something to bill are offered); the concepts without line are
   proposed again on the new orders.
5. Review the mapping: change the **Purchase Order Line** of a concept
   or its **Quantity to Bill** (the CFDI quantity, converted to the unit
   of the order line when the SAT unit code is known). The quantities to
   bill cannot exceed the pending quantity of each order line. A concept
   without order line blocks the creation of the bill unless **Allow
   concepts without order** is ticked and a product is selected on it;
   it is then billed as a free line.
6. Click **Create Bill**. The vendor bill is created in draft with one
   line per concept (product, taxes and analytic distribution of the
   order line; quantity, unit price and discount of the CFDI), the
   orders as origin, the CFDI serie and folio as reference, its date as
   bill date and the XML attached. A warning is shown in the wizard when
   the amount to bill differs from the CFDI subtotal, and a note is
   posted in the bill when its total differs from the CFDI total.

The **Not billed** filter of **SAT > Documents** lists the received
CFDIs that still have no vendor bill.
